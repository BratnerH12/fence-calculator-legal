Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you use Fence Calculator to plan fences, estimate materials and costs, and manage projects.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the privacy practices of Fence Calculator ("the App," "we," or "us"). This policy explains the information used to provide and support the App, including information handled by service providers.

1. Information stored on your device

The App lets you create fence layouts, configure fence specifications, enter material prices, generate estimates, and save projects. Project details and preferences are designed to be stored locally on your device. The current version does not require a separate in-app account to buy or restore a subscription.

Information you enter may include project names, fence dimensions, gate placements, map or canvas layouts, material quantities, prices, and notes. If you choose to export or share a PDF, the destination and recipients are controlled by the sharing options you select.

2. Purchases and subscription access

Subscriptions are purchased and billed through Apple's App Store. We use RevenueCat to check purchase status, manage subscription entitlements, and support restoring purchases. RevenueCat and Apple may process purchase records, subscription status, transaction identifiers, an app-specific or anonymous user identifier, and technical device information necessary to provide these services.

We do not receive your full payment card information from Apple. For information about these providers' practices, see Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

3. Maps and location

When you choose to use location-based or map-based planning tools, the App may use your selected location, map searches, or location permissions to display and work with a map. Location access is controlled through your device settings. You can decline permission; certain location-based features may then be unavailable.

To deliver map and place-search features, the relevant map or location service provider may receive information necessary to complete your request, such as a map search, coordinates, device network information, or an approximate location. Those services handle information under their own privacy practices.

4. App analytics and error diagnostics

We use Aptabase to understand how people use the App and identify areas for improvement. Our analytics events record actions such as starting the App, creating a project, completing or failing a calculation, preparing a layout, or exporting a PDF. Aptabase also processes technical information such as the App version, operating system, device model, language, event time, and a short-lived session identifier. It derives an approximate country and region from the internet connection's IP address; according to Aptabase, it does not store that IP address or use persistent device identifiers.

Our analytics events are designed not to contain project names, fence dimensions, material prices, project notes, addresses, or GPS coordinates. For more information, see Aptabase's Privacy Policy.

We use Sentry to identify and fix crashes, application errors, and other technical problems. Error reports may include the error message, stack trace, app version, device and operating-system details, timestamps, and diagnostic context. We have configured Sentry not to send default personally identifying information, and we have disabled session replay, performance tracing, profiling, and additional application logging. We do not deliberately attach fence projects or location details to reports, although technical errors can sometimes contain unexpected information. For more information, see Sentry's Privacy Policy.

5. How information is used

  • To calculate fence measurements, material quantities, and estimated costs.
  • To save and reopen your locally stored projects and preferences.
  • To display maps or use location features when requested.
  • To verify an active subscription, process purchases, and restore access.
  • To understand general feature usage and improve the App through privacy-conscious analytics.
  • To diagnose crashes, correct software errors, and maintain reliability.
  • To respond to support requests you choose to send us.

We do not sell personal information or use the App to display third-party targeted advertisements.

6. Service providers

We use Apple for App Store distribution, purchases, and device services; RevenueCat for subscription management; Aptabase for app analytics; and Sentry for crash and error diagnostics. The App also uses mapping and location services, including Geoapify for address search suggestions. When you use address search, the search text and technical information needed to process the request may be sent to Geoapify. These providers process information according to their own privacy practices and applicable agreements. See Geoapify's Privacy Policy.

7. Storage, retention, and deletion

You can manage and delete saved projects through the App's available controls. Removing the App typically removes app data stored locally on the device, subject to any device or system backups. Purchase and subscription records may remain with Apple and RevenueCat according to their retention requirements and policies. Analytics data and technical error reports are retained by Aptabase and Sentry under their respective retention policies and settings. Aptabase states that its analytics data may be retained for up to five years and that its short-lived identifiers do not allow events to be connected to a person across days.

For questions about personal data processed in connection with the App, contact us using the address below. We will assess requests to access or delete information we control, as required by applicable law.

8. Security

We seek to use appropriate security measures for information processed to operate the App. No device, transmission, or storage method is completely secure. Protect your device and its backups with appropriate security settings.

9. Children's privacy

The App is intended for general fence-planning use and is not designed specifically for children. We do not intentionally solicit children's personal information. If you have a concern, contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when the App or its data practices change. The revised policy will be available on this page with an updated date.
